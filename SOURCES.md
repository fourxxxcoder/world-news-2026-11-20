# Sources — the news of Friday 20 November 2026

Every story on the poster was reported on 20 November 2026. The
poster names the day in its masthead and keeps each claim short so
it stays faithful to the reporting.

## Hero

- **World Court rules Myanmar committed genocide against the
  Rohingya.** The International Court of Justice held a state
  responsible for genocide for the first time, by 9 votes to 6, in
  the case brought by The Gambia over the 2017 Rakhine operation.
  More than 1.3 million Rohingya remain displaced.
  Reported by BBC News, PBS NewsHour and UN News.

## Panels

- **Trump, Xi and Putin hold first summit.** The US, Chinese and
  Russian leaders met in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the APEC
  summit and signed a four-point joint declaration covering
  nuclear risk, UN reform, energy supply and the ICC. Analysts
  reported no breakthrough on Ukraine.
  Reported by BBC News, The Guardian and Euronews.
- **Russia pounds Kyiv.** A massive overnight missile and drone
  attack on 19–20 November killed eight people, injured 28 across
  nine regions and hit three bridges in Kyiv. Ukraine struck the
  Kerch (Crimea) Bridge for the third time in a week, and Putin
  vowed a painful response.
  Reported by AP, BBC News, Reuters and CNN.
- **Missile damages Riyadh tower.** Houthi missiles hit Riyadh's
  financial district and damaged the Public Investment Fund tower;
  Saudi Arabia also intercepted missiles and drones. The Iran war
  is in its ninth month, with tanker attacks in the Strait of
  Hormuz raising oil-supply risk.
  Reported by Sky News, Middle East Eye and The Hindu.
- **Stocks tumble as the AI boom is tested.** Anthropic closed
  17% above its $83 offer price (around $97) in a record Nasdaq
  debut, yet the S&P 500 fell about 2% for a fifth straight day,
  Japan's Nikkei fell 4.8% and gold fell 4.2%.
  Reported by BBC News, CNBC, Al Jazeera and The Hindu.
- **COP31 draft drops the fossil-fuel exit.** Negotiators in
  Antalya removed the call to phase out fossil fuels from the
  draft deal; Pacific nations demanded a phase-out as the talks
  entered their final day.
  Reported by Japan Times, CBC News and The Hindu.
- **Voyager 1 reaches one light-day.** NASA's Voyager 1, launched
  in 1977, reached a distance of one light-day — more than
  26 billion kilometres — 49 years after launch. The same week
  the European-Japanese BepiColombo probe reached Mercury orbit.
  Reported by NASA and France 24.

## Also today (footer)

- **Kenya beats Ebola.** Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient,
  37 days after its first case, a containment commentators credit
  to preparation. Reported by AllAfrica.
- **GTA 6 launch tops $2.3 billion.** Grand Theft Auto VI went on
  sale worldwide after 13 years; industry estimates put sales
  above $2.3 billion, expected to be the biggest entertainment
  launch ever. Reported by BBC Business.
- **UK and EU seal a youth mobility deal.** Up to 100,000 young
  people a year would be allowed to travel, work and study across
  the UK and the EU, alongside food-trade and emissions deals.
  Reported by BBC News, Reuters and The Guardian.
- **US Senate passes energy permitting bill 62–33.** The
  bipartisan bill speeds approvals for power plants, transmission,
  mines and data centres and now goes to the House.
  Reported by Bloomberg, AFP and NBC News.

## Method

Stories were chosen for consequence and breadth — justice,
diplomacy, two wars, markets and AI, climate, space, health,
culture and politics — across The Hague, Shenzhen, Kyiv, Riyadh,
New York, Antalya, Nairobi and deep space. Numbers are the ones
reported that day; estimates (GTA 6 sales) are described as the
day's reported figures in the coverage above.
